# Algebraic Topology

Notes of a course in NTHU, 2026 Fall.
Main text: W. S. Massey, *A Basic Course in Algebraic Topology* (GTM 127).

| Chapter | Topics |
|---|---|
| [1. Surfaces](ch01-surfaces.md) | manifolds, quotient topology, $\mathbb{RP}^2$, connected sums, classification of compact surfaces, Euler characteristic, orientation |
| [2. Fundamental groups](ch02-fundamental-groups.md) | paths, homotopy, reparametrization, products of paths, the fundamental group $\pi(X,x)$, induced homomorphisms, retracts and deformation retracts, $\pi(S^1)\cong\mathbb{Z}$, Brouwer |
| [3. Free groups and free products](ch03-free-groups.md) | direct products, the weak product, universality of the direct sum, free abelian groups, $\mathbb{Z}\langle S\rangle$, free products, free groups, reduced words |
| [4. The Seifert–Van Kampen theorem](ch04-van-kampen.md) | statement and generalization, the generating lemma (Lebesgue number), $\pi(U)*\pi(V)$ when $U\cap V$ is simply connected, $\pi(U)/N$ when $V$ is simply connected, bouquets of circles, $S^2$ minus points, the torus |
| [5. Covering spaces](ch05-covering-spaces.md) | covering spaces and examples ($\mathbb{R}\to S^1$, $z\mapsto z^n$, $S^2\to\mathbb{RP}^2$, coverings of the figure eight), lifting of paths, covering homotopy property, $p_*$ injective, conjugacy classes, homomorphisms and automorphisms, regular coverings, group actions on the fibre, Borsuk–Ulam |
